# Simple app text

## NDVI
NDVI is a satellite measure of plant greenness. High/green usually means healthy vegetation; low can mean bare soil, water, or a flooded field.

## Crop rotation
Crop rotation means growing different crops in the same field in different seasons. It can reduce repeated pest pressure and improve soil management.

## Legume
A legume is a crop such as soybean that can work with soil bacteria to add nitrogen. It may support soil fertility, but local management still matters.
